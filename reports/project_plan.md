## Project Objective:

To analyse customer retention behaviour and calculate Customer Lifetime Value (CLTV) using e-commerce transaction data and business analytics techniques. The project aims to identify customer retention patterns, customer segments, churn behaviour, and long-term customer value through advanced data analytics and visualisation techniques.

## Dataset

### Selected Dataset

Online Retail II Dataset

### Dataset Selection Process

* Researched multiple datasets from Kaggle, UCI Repository, and public data sources.
* Compared datasets based on project requirements and business objectives.
* Evaluated transaction-based e-commerce datasets suitable for customer analytics.
* Selected the Online Retail II Dataset as the primary dataset due to its suitability for Cohort Analysis, Customer Retention Analysis, CLTV Analysis, RFM Segmentation, and Churn Analysis.
