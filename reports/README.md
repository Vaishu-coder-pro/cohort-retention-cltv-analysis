
Customer Analytics Dashboard & Business Intelligence Reports

About the Project

This project was completed as part of a Data Science Internship using the Brazilian E-commerce (Olist) dataset. The main objective was to analyse customer behaviour, customer lifetime value (CLTV), retention patterns, product performance, and churn risk, and present the results through interactive Power BI dashboards.

My role in the project focused on dashboard development, business visualization, report preparation, and presenting analytical results in a clear and meaningful way. Working with my teammate, I helped convert complex analytical outputs into interactive dashboards that make it easier for business users and management to understand customer trends and make informed decisions.
